Storm preparation around the home

Four practical places to start before the next downpour.

01

Test the whole sump-pump cycle

When conditions are dry and it is safe, confirm that the float starts the pump, the water level drops, and water leaves through the discharge point.

02

Keep discharge water away

A line that ends too close to the foundation, leaks, freezes shut, or sends water toward a low spot can bring it right back.

03

Check the water coming from above

Clear gutters, repair obvious leaks, and make sure downspouts and surface grading carry roof water away from the house.

04

Plan for the failure point

Power loss, a stuck float, return flow, a blocked line, and sewer backup can all defeat an otherwise working system. Ask which safeguards fit your home.

The Common Questions

Before you call.

A few of the questions we hear most. If yours isn’t here, the phone is the fastest way through.

How fast can you get out here?
Call to discuss current availability. For active leaks, water intrusion, sewer backup, or no-water, tell us when you call so we can understand the urgency.
Are you licensed and insured in Indiana?
David Kropczynski holds an active Indiana Plumbing Contractor credential. Indiana licenses: Plumbing Contractor PC12300114 (David Kropczynski); Plumbing Corporation CO52600043 (Simple Man Plumbing). Ask about the documentation and scope that apply to your specific project.
Do you give an upfront price before you start?
We explain the work and provide clear pricing before you decide how to proceed. Ask for the current estimate for your repair or installation.
How do payment options work?
We accept all major credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex, Discover), ACH transfers, and personal checks upon completion. For larger installations and staged projects, milestone payment terms can be written directly into the proposal.
Can I call about an urgent water problem?
Call to discuss current availability for an urgent leak, water intrusion, sewer backup, or no-water issue.
What warranty terms apply?
Every job carries a one-year workmanship guarantee. If something we installed or repaired fails because of how the work was done, we come back and make it right at no additional labor charge. Manufacturer warranties on parts and fixtures are separate and vary by product — ask and we’ll tell you what applies to yours.
