On the Ground in Greenwood

What we actually see in Greenwood homes.

Greenwood anchors the south side in Johnson County, where the brick streets of Old Town Greenwood meet wave after wave of newer subdivisions stretching toward Center Grove. It’s a true mix of established homes and recent construction, and we cover both the same way.

The older core has its share of mid-century supply lines and drains ready for attention, while the 1990s-and-newer subdivisions out toward Center Grove can carry polybutylene from that era alongside builder-grade water heaters now timing out. Renovation work is common here — we re-plumb kitchens and baths to match what owners are updating above the floor.

Clay soil and finished basements make sump pumps and backups a standard recommendation across Greenwood. The hard Central Indiana water that scales heaters and clouds fixtures runs down here too, so softener service stays steady year-round.

Flood readiness

Flood prevention and sump-pump help in Greenwood.

Greenwood’s clay soil and finished basements make a reliable sump system especially important when storms stack up. We can test the pump, install or replace equipment, and check how the discharge line carries water away from the foundation.

For heavy-rain preparation, we can talk through backup-system options and assess wet-basement, drainage, sewer-backup, or backwater-valve concerns. The right mix depends on the home, its lower level, and what the plumbing is already telling you.
