About Simple Man.

We treat every visit like it’s our own house — because most of our calls come from neighbors who saw the truck on the next street over.

An Introduction

Dave. Owner.

On the tools for over eight years. Still answering the phone, still writing up every job by hand before leaving the driveway.

I started this practice because I couldn’t find a plumber who treated my mother’s house the way I’d treat my own. Twenty-some years on, the standard hasn’t moved: show up when I say I will, explain what I’m doing in language a homeowner actually understands, leave the space cleaner than I found it.

If we’re working in your home, you’ll have my cell number before I leave. That’s the simplest way I know to mean what I say.

— Dave
Owner & Lead Plumber · Indiana Plumbing Contractor

Years of hands-on work.

Built one job at a time, on the Northside and across greater Indianapolis.

The Story

Doing things the right way — the simple way.

At Simple Man Plumbing, we believe in doing things the right way — the simple way. With years of hands-on experience in residential plumbing, we’ve built a reputation for being reliable, transparent, and always on time. From fixing leaky faucets to full-scale plumbing installations, we’ve done it all.

The name says most of what we believe. We are simple men doing honest work. We don’t pad invoices, we don’t invent problems, and we don’t leave a job until you’d be glad to have us back.

Our service area runs from Greenwood up through downtown Indianapolis and into the Northside — Carmel, Fishers, Westfield, Noblesville, and out east to Pendleton. The size of the job changes; the standard does not.
